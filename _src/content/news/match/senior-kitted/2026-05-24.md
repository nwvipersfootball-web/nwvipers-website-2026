## Club Statement
The Northwest Vipers Committee has been informed by American Football Ireland that this Sunday’s Senior Kitted fixture against the Cill Dara Crusaders will no longer take place following notification that the Crusaders have forfeited the remainder of their season.

In accordance with league regulations, the result will be recorded as a 30-0 victory for the Northwest Vipers, moving the team to 5-1 on the season.

While we were looking forward to taking the field this weekend, our focus now turns to the next stage of the season. We look forward to facing the Crusaders in the future and wish them the best moving forward.

Thank you to our players, staff, supporters and sponsors for your continued backing throughout the season.
