# Young Vipers Push Combined Side All the Way in Narrow Defeat

## Opening Overview
The Northwest Vipers rounded off the opening Youth Blitz of the 2026 season with an encouraging display against a combined Causeway Giants and West Dublin Rhinos side. Having gained valuable experience in their opening game, the young Vipers showed significant improvement in Game 2, pushing their opponents all the way in a hard-fought contest. Although the Vipers narrowly fell short, the performance highlighted the rapid development of a squad featuring many players making their first competitive appearances.

## First Half Summary
The combined Giants and Rhinos side made a quick start, finding the end zone early before adding a second score to take control of the first half. Despite the early deficit, the Vipers continued to compete with tremendous determination, refusing to let the game slip away.

Defensively, Enda Boyce was outstanding, repeatedly breaking through the offensive line to register a number of sacks and disrupt the opposition's rhythm. Around him, Harry Burgess, Noah Campbell, Joel Campbell, and Dominic Donaghy-Rees all produced big tackles throughout the half, ensuring every yard was hard earned. The defensive effort kept the Vipers within striking distance heading into the break despite trailing on the scoreboard.

## Second Half Summary
The Vipers produced their strongest football of the day after halftime, growing in confidence on both sides of the ball. Quarterback Peter Danowski once again led from the front, using his legs to punch in the Vipers' only touchdown of the contest and ignite the team's comeback.

The Vipers weren't finished there. Peter Danowski followed up by successfully running in the two-point conversion, reducing the deficit to just six points and setting up a tense finish. The defence continued to stand tall, with Enda Boyce applying relentless pressure and the tackling of Harry Burgess, Noah Campbell, Joel Campbell, and Dominic Donaghy-Rees giving the combined side little room to operate.

Despite a determined finish and several defensive stops, the Vipers were unable to find the score that would level the game, eventually falling just short in a spirited 14–8 defeat.


## Key Performers
Peter Danowski once again led by example, scoring the Vipers' touchdown before adding the two-point conversion to keep his side firmly in contention. Enda Boyce delivered an excellent defensive performance, recording multiple sacks and causing problems for the opposition throughout the game. Harry Burgess, Noah Campbell, Joel Campbell, and Dominic Donaghy-Rees all impressed with a series of powerful tackles, underlining the physicality and determination of a defence that continued to improve as the day progressed.

## Closing & Next Fixture
While the Vipers finished the opening blitz with two defeats, the growth shown across both games was impossible to ignore. With many players experiencing competitive American football for the first time, the improvement from Game 1 to Game 2 was a testament to the attitude and character of this young squad. The foundations have been laid for an exciting season, and the team will return to training full of confidence as they prepare for the next Youth Blitz.
