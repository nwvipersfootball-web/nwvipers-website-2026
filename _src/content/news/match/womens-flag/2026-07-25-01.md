# Northwest Vipers Open Fianna Bowl Campaign with Dominant Wild Card Victory

![Vipers in action]({{ image | prepend: '/assets/images/news'  }})

| Date | Saturday July 25th, 2026 |
| Gameweek | AFI Women's Flag Fianna Bowl Wilcard Round|
| Opponent | Belfast Knights  |
| Venue | St. Mary's RFC, Dublin |
| Final Score | 30-7 |
| Season Record | 4-10 |

## Opening Overview
The Northwest Vipers Women's Flag Team got their Fianna Bowl playoff campaign off to the perfect start with a commanding 30-7 victory over the Belfast Knights in Saturday morning's Wild Card clash.

With a place in the Fianna Bowl Semi-Final against the tournament's No. 1 seed, the Louth Mavericks, at stake, the Vipers delivered one of their most complete performances of the season. Dominant on both sides of the ball, they forced turnovers, made big plays when it mattered, and never allowed the Knights to build any sustained momentum as they punched their ticket into the last four.

## First Half Summary
The Vipers defence wasted little time making its mark as Rachel Reed opened the game with a crucial interception, setting the tone for what would become a dominant defensive display.

Quarterback Cara Phillips capitalised almost immediately, launching a perfectly placed Hail Mary pass that was brilliantly hauled in by Tia Sadlon for the opening touchdown of the contest.

Momentum continued to swing heavily in the Vipers' favour as Phillips showcased her versatility by coming up with an interception of her own, giving the offence another opportunity to extend the lead.

The offence remained composed and clinical, with Phillips once again finding the end zone, this time connecting with Aisling Grieve for another Vipers touchdown. At halftime, the Vipers were firmly in control, combining disciplined defence with explosive offensive plays to put themselves in an excellent position.

## Second Half Summary
Any hopes of a Belfast comeback were quickly extinguished after the break.

Cara Phillips produced one of the standout moments of the game, reading the quarterback perfectly before stepping in front of the pass and returning it all the way for a Pick Six touchdown to stretch the Vipers' advantage.

The offence continued to move the ball efficiently, with Amy Gorham adding another score before Rachel Reed capped off an outstanding all-round performance by crossing the goal line herself.

Although the Knights managed to register a late consolation touchdown, it did little to disrupt the Vipers' momentum as they comfortably closed out an impressive 30-7 victory and booked their place in the Fianna Bowl Semi-Finals.

## Key Performers
Cara Phillips starred on both sides of the ball, throwing touchdown passes to Tia Sadlon and Aisling Grieve while also recording an interception and a Pick Six in a superb all-round performance.

Rachel Reed was outstanding defensively, opening the game with an interception before adding a touchdown of her own, while Tia Sadlon's spectacular Hail Mary catch proved to be one of the biggest plays of the game.

Amy Gorham and Aisling Grieve both found the end zone, while the Vipers' defence consistently applied pressure and forced turnovers to control the contest from start to finish.

## Closing & Next Fixture
The victory sends the Northwest Vipers into the Fianna Bowl Semi-Final, where they will face the tournament's top seed, the Louth Mavericks, later in the day.

After a dominant Wild Card performance filled with defensive takeaways and explosive offensive plays, the Vipers will take plenty of confidence into the next challenge as they continue their pursuit of the 2026 Fianna Bowl Championship.
