# Heartbreak for Northwest Vipers as Fianna Bowl Final Ends in One-Point Defeat

![Vipers in action]({{ image | prepend: '/assets/images/news'  }})

## Opening Overview
The Northwest Vipers Women's Flag Team came within a single point of Fianna Bowl glory on Saturday afternoon, falling 26-25 to the Dublin City Wolves in what many across the American Football Ireland community described as the game of the year.

In a thrilling championship contest that swung back and forth from the opening drive until the final whistle, both teams traded scores throughout an unforgettable final. The Vipers left everything on the field, producing a performance full of heart, resilience and determination, but ultimately came up just short in one of the most dramatic finishes of the season.

## First Half Summary
The Vipers made the perfect start as quarterback Cara Phillips connected with Rachel Reed for the opening touchdown to give Northwest an early 6-0 advantage.

The Wolves responded to level the contest before the Vipers struck again through one of the biggest plays of the afternoon. Amy Gorham intercepted a pass and took it all the way back to the house, restoring the Vipers' lead and sending the travelling support into celebration.

Once again, the Wolves had an answer, matching the Vipers score for score in an entertaining first half. By the halftime whistle, the championship decider was finely poised with the teams locked at 13-13.

## Second Half Summary
The momentum continued to swing throughout the second half as neither side was willing to give an inch.

Cara Phillips and Rachel Reed combined once more, with Phillips finding Reed for her second touchdown reception of the afternoon to put the Vipers back in front.

The Wolves immediately responded, setting up a dramatic finish before the Vipers once again regained the lead. Reed completed a sensational individual performance by hauling in her third touchdown of the game, giving Northwest every chance of lifting the Fianna Bowl trophy.

The Wolves managed to edge ahead 26-25 late in the contest, leaving the Vipers with one final opportunity to win the championship.

Rachel Reed gave the offence that opportunity with a huge interception in the closing stages, putting the ball back into Vipers hands for one last drive. Marching down the field, Northwest faced a decisive fourth down, but an incomplete pass intended for Amy Gorham brought the drive to an end, allowing the Wolves to run out the clock and secure a dramatic one-point victory.

## Key Performers
Rachel Reed produced an outstanding championship performance, catching three touchdown passes and recording a crucial late interception that gave the Vipers one final chance to win the title.

Cara Phillips led the offence superbly, throwing all three touchdown passes to Reed while keeping the Vipers in front for large periods of the contest.

Amy Gorham delivered one of the plays of the game with a pick-six touchdown that kept the momentum firmly with Northwest in a final that showcased the very best of women's flag football.

## Closing
Although the result wasn't the one the Northwest Vipers had hoped for, the team's remarkable playoff run will be remembered for years to come.

After defeating the Belfast Knights in the Wild Card round and stunning the top-seeded Louth Mavericks in the semi-final, the Vipers pushed the Dublin City Wolves all the way in a championship final that many have already labelled the game of the season.

Falling just one point short is a difficult way to end the campaign, but the character, quality and belief shown throughout the Fianna Bowl playoffs has firmly established the Northwest Vipers as one of the premier teams in the American Football Ireland Women's Flag League.
