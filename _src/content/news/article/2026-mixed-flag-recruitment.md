# Come and join the Youth Kitted squad

## Training
Starting June 20th at 10am until 12pm at the Vale Centre, Greysteel. Then training will be every Saturday with the first gameday taking place in August. Games are played on Saturdays.

## Matches
All matches are played on Saturdays. The AFI Mixed Flag Schedule has not be confirmed yet. 

## What you'll need
Come ready to train with boots and water. We'll have some kit to loan out to get you going.

## Club Fees
Club fees for mixed flag are £150 for those aged over 18. For those aged under 18, fees are £50 for the year.

## Get in Touch
No experience necessary, so come and see what it's all about. [Join us](/contact/).
