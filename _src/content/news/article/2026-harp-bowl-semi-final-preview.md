# Northwest Vipers Set for Harp Bowl Semi-Final Showdown Against West Dublin Rhinos

## Overview
Te Northwest Vipers are calling on the entire community to get behind the team as they host the biggest game in the club's history to date.

On **Saturday 4th July**, the Vipers welcome the **West Dublin Rhinos to City of Derry RFC** for the **AFI Division One Harp Bowl Semi-Final**, with kick-off scheduled for **2:00pm.**

With a place in the **Harp Bowl Championship Game in Athlone on 18th July at stake**, everything is on the line in what promises to be an exciting afternoon of American football.

## Family Fun Day
This is much more than just a football game. The Vipers are hosting a Family Fun Day, making it the perfect afternoon out for supporters of all ages.

On the day there will be:

Food vendors
Open bar
Bouncy castles
Family entertainment
And much more throughout the afternoon

Whether you're a lifelong American football fan or completely new to the sport, everyone is welcome to come along and experience one of the fastest-growing sports in Ireland.

## Match Preview

The Vipers enter the playoffs after an outstanding regular season, finishing with a **7-1 record and earning home advantage for the semi-final**.

Standing in their way are the **West Dublin Rhinos**, one of the strongest sides in Division One. With both teams just one win away from the championship game, expect a physical, high-intensity contest from the opening whistle.

The stakes could not be higher. The winner will book their place in the Harp Bowl Championship in Athlone on 18th July, while the losing team's season comes to an end.

With home support behind them, the Vipers will be hoping to make the most of the occasion and continue what has been a remarkable campaign.

## Get Behind the Vipers
This is a landmark day for American football in the North West and a huge occasion for the club. The players have worked tirelessly all season to earn the opportunity to compete for a place in the Harp Bowl, and now they need your support.

Bring your family, bring your friends, enjoy everything the Family Fun Day has to offer, and help create an unforgettable atmosphere as the Northwest Vipers battle for a place in the championship game.
